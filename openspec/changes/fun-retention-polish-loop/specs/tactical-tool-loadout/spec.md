## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: VIP Tool Benefits Are Applied
The tactical loadout SHALL apply VIP extra slots and scanner/reveal radius bonuses to actual gameplay behavior.

#### Scenario: VIP extra slot
- **WHEN** a player has a VIP tier with extra loadout slots
- **THEN** the play tool bar exposes the increased number of tactical tools.

#### Scenario: VIP scanner radius
- **WHEN** a player has scanner radius bonus
- **THEN** scanner/reveal visibility uses the enhanced radius.
