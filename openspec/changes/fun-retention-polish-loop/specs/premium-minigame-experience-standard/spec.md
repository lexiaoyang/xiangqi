## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Retention-Grade Mini-Game Loop
A premium mini-game experience SHALL provide visible immediate goals, daily return goals, long-term mastery goals, honest monetization value, and polished feedback for major actions.

#### Scenario: Player returns next day
- **WHEN** a returning player opens the hub
- **THEN** they see at least one daily goal, one long-term progression target, and a clear primary action.
