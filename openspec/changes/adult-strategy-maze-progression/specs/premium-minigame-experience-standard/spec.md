## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Adult Strategic Depth Standard
Player-facing gameplay SHALL meet an adult strategic depth standard: levels must require planning, risk assessment, resource decisions, or objective sequencing after the tutorial band.

#### Scenario: Post-tutorial level review
- **WHEN** a level after the beginner band is generated
- **THEN** it includes at least one strategic layer beyond simple corridor navigation.

### Requirement: Monetization Value Clarity Standard
Paid, ad, VIP, and reward surfaces SHALL explain gameplay value in terms of tools, strategy, recovery, mastery, or convenience.

#### Scenario: Product card displayed
- **WHEN** a shop, ad, or VIP product is visible
- **THEN** it describes the concrete gameplay benefit and exact reward contents.

### Requirement: Non-Trivial But Fair Standard
The game SHALL avoid one-look triviality while preserving fairness, solvability, and visible rules.

#### Scenario: Complex mechanics are active
- **WHEN** multiple mechanics are active in one level
- **THEN** the HUD or prep brief explains active rules and the level remains solvable without paid consumables.
